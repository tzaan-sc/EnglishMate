Chủ ngữ (Subject) Dạng Khẳng định Dạng Phủ định (Rút gọn)
I am (I'm) am not (I'm not)
He / She / It is (He's / She's / It's) is not (isn't)
You / We / They are (You're / We're / They're) are not (aren't)
Ghi nhớ dạng câu hỏi & trả lời ngắn:
  • Câu hỏi Yes/No: Am / Is / Are + S + Complement?
